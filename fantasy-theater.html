What does a budget get me?

Which floor should you claim at?

Every failed exploration pays 10 tokens per amulet, so claiming at Floors 2–5 comes out about even. Higher floors only pull ahead once you have enough amulets to finish expensive climbs and collect Fanstars for Fortress runs. With few amulets you risk running out mid-climb, which is why Floor 2 wins at small budgets. The best value in each column is highlighted. "Long run" is an exact calculation for unlimited amulets.
